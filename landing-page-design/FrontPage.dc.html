Corrections & Amplifications

A note on editorial policy

This paper amplifies before it corrects. How much is up to you, set once, adjustable any time:

Low — Warm and wholesome. The paper is fond of you. Safe to leave on the kitchen table.

Medium — Light teasing, a raised eyebrow in the copy. Friendly, but it noticed. This is the house style.

High — Sharper roasting, right to the line — aimed only at you. The paper never runs real people, and never punches at anyone else.

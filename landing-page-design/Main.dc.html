SPORTSCENTER · Just now
It was supposed to be a routine possession. Mug in the right hand, laptop bag on the left shoulder, a clean lane to the front door. Then, with the clock showing 8:51 and tip-off at 9:00, Rahman lost the handle. The coffee hit the floor. The shoes took the splash. Officials are still reviewing whether the travel violation happened before or after the spill, but the outcome was never in doubt: this drive ended in a turnover, and the crowd — one cat, unbothered — had seen enough. "You practice that carry a thousand times," said a source close to the kitchen. "And then the one morning it matters, the fundamentals just aren't there." Rahman recovered to reach the office by 9:14, but the tape doesn't lie. He'll want this one back.

How sharp you want it

You set the edge.

One setting decides how hard the correspondents come for you. It only ever points one direction.

Low

Warm and wholesome. Plays your day for charm, never at your expense. Safe to read to your mom.

Medium Default

Light teasing and a raised eyebrow. Friendly overall — but it definitely noticed.

High

Sharper roasting, right up to the line. It comes for you and only you — never real people, never anyone else.

Feed it anything

However you take notes.

Type it

A paragraph, a list, or one bitter sentence. The worse your notes, the harder the correspondents work.

Talk it

Hit the mic and narrate the walk home. It transcribes, then files.

Show it

Screenshot the calendar, the text thread, the receipt. It reads the room.

Before you ask

The obvious questions.

Does it keep my entries?

Past broadcasts are saved in your browser so you can reread them. Nothing leaves your machine except the notes you send to generate a segment.

Will it be mean about my actual friends?

No. Even on High, the jokes point at you. Real people never get roasted.

Do I have to write well?

No. Fragments, typos, and one-word moods are all fine. That's the correspondents' problem now.

Is this journaling or a bit?

Both. You still wrote down your day — it just came back with a camera crew.
